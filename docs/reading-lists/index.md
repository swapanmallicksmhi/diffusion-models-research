# Reading Roadmap

## Stage 1 — Foundations

Read [Ho et al. (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239) to understand Gaussian denoising and training objectives.

## Stage 2 — Sampling improvements

Read [DDIM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02502) and [Improved DDPM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09672).

## Stage 3 — Score-based theory

Read [Song et al. (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.13456) for SDEs, reverse-time sampling and probability flow.

## Stage 4 — Data assimilation

Read [Rozet & Louppe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10574), then [DiffDA](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/huang24h.html), followed by [kilometer-scale weather DA](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024MS004505).

## Stage 5 — Research synthesis

For each paper, write an independent note on its assumptions, inference algorithm, observations, evaluation, reproducibility and unresolved questions.
