**Authors:** Jan-Hendrik Bastek, WaiChing Sun, and Dennis M. Kochmann

**Publication:** 13th International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2025)

**Year:** 2025

**Research category:** Physics-Informed Generative Modeling

### Scientific Overview

This study introduces a physics-informed diffusion modeling
framework that incorporates governing physical equations
into the generative learning objective.

A physics-based loss term is introduced to encourage
generated samples to satisfy prescribed physical
constraints, including partial differential equations.

The framework is evaluated using fluid flow problems
and structural topology optimization.

### Main Scientific Contributions

- Integration of physics-based constraints into diffusion
  model training.
- Improved consistency of generated samples with
  governing physical equations.
- Application to fluid flow and topology optimization.
- Investigation of the regularization effects of
  physics-informed training objectives.

### Relevance to Earth System Sciences

Physics-informed diffusion models offer a potential
framework for incorporating physical constraints into
generative atmospheric and Earth system modeling.

The methodology is relevant to the development of
physically consistent generative models, although
application to operational numerical weather prediction
requires further investigation.
